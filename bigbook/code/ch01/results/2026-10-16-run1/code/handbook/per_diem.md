# Per-diem and lodging (fictional ACME Corp. handbook, 2026)

Meals per-diem is paid per person per travel day. Lodging is reimbursed at cost up to the nightly cap.

| City    | Meals per-diem (USD/day) | Lodging cap (USD/night) |
|---------|--------------------------|-------------------------|
| Austin  | 64                       | 185                     |
| Boston  | 79                       | 265                     |
| Chicago | 74                       | 230                     |
| Denver  | 68                       | 195                     |
| Seattle | 76                       | 240                     |

Cities not listed here have no approved rate; ask Finance before booking.
