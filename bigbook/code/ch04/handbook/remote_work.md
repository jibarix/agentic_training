# Remote work (fictional ACME Corp. handbook, 2026)

## 1. Eligibility

Employees may work from home up to 3 days a week after they complete their probation period of 3 months. Teams set their own office days; the default is Tuesday and Thursday.

## 2. Home-office stipend

Remote workers receive a one-time home-office stipend of 500 USD for a desk, a chair and lighting, and 35 USD per month toward internet costs.

## 3. Working abroad

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per year, with approval from your manager and from HR at least 30 days in advance.

## 4. Office hours

All offices are open 08:00-19:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are 10:00-15:00 in the employee's home office time zone.
