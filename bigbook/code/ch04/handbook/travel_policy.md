# Travel policy (fictional ACME Corp. handbook, 2026)

## 1. Booking

Book flights and hotels through the travel desk at least 14 days before departure. Economy class is the standard for every flight under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Business and first class are not reimbursed, whatever the length of the flight. A trip booked later than 7 days before departure needs your manager's written approval.

## 2. Meals per-diem

ACME pays a fixed meals per-diem for each travel day, at the rate for the destination city in per_diem.md. The day you leave and the day you return each count as a full travel day. No receipts are needed for meals. Meals that a client or a conference provides are still covered by the per-diem; you do not have to deduct them.

## 3. Lodging

Hotels are reimbursed at cost, per night, up to the lodging cap for the city in per_diem.md. If the hotel charges more than the cap, ACME pays the cap and you pay the difference. Taxes count toward the cap. A trip of one day has no nights and no lodging.

## 4. Mileage

Driving on company business is reimbursed per mile at the rate for the vehicle in mileage.md. Count the full distance driven, including the return. Commuting between home and your usual office is not reimbursable. Parking and tolls are reimbursed at cost with a receipt.

## 5. Client-billed trips

Some trips are billed to a client under the client's contract. ACME does not reimburse those trips: meals, lodging and mileage on them are claimed from the client, through the account manager. The trip log marks each trip as billed to ACME or to the client.

## 6. Claims and deadlines

Submit each claim within 30 days of the trip's last day. Claims submitted later than 90 days are not paid, except with approval from the Finance director. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll after approval.
