# Information security (fictional ACME Corp. handbook, 2026)

## 1. Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must have at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is required for every company account. Never reuse a company password on another site.

## 2. Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT service desk within 24 hours. IT will lock and erase the device remotely.

## 3. Reporting incidents

Report a suspected phishing email or security incident to security@acme.example at once. Do not forward the suspicious message to colleagues.

## 4. Customer data

Customer data may be stored only on company systems. Copying it to a personal device or a personal cloud account is a disciplinary offence.
