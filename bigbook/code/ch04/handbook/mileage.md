# Mileage rates (fictional ACME Corp. handbook, 2026)

Rates per mile driven on company business, by vehicle. The trip log records the total miles driven on each trip, including the return.

| Vehicle      | Rate (USD/mile) |
|--------------|-----------------|
| car          | 0.70            |
| electric car | 0.62            |
| van          | 0.85            |
| motorcycle   | 0.45            |

Trips by plane or train with no vehicle ("none" in the trip log) have no mileage.
