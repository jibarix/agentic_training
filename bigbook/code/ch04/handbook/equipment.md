# Equipment (fictional ACME Corp. handbook, 2026)

## 1. Laptops

Each new employee receives a laptop budget of 1,800 USD. The budget is raised by 4 percent every year, compounding, starting in 2027. Laptops are replaced every 4 years, or sooner if IT finds them beyond repair.

## 2. Monitors and accessories

Up to 2 monitors per employee, 250 USD each. A keyboard, a mouse and a headset are provided on request, up to 150 USD in total.

## 3. Mobile phones

Employees who are on call receive a company phone. Others may claim 40 USD per month toward a personal phone plan used for work.

## 4. Returning equipment

All equipment remains ACME property. Return it to IT within 5 working days of your last day. The cost of equipment that is not returned is deducted from the final salary payment.
