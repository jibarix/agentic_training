# Leave (fictional ACME Corp. handbook, 2026)

## 1. Public and floating holidays

Employees receive 11 paid public holidays and 2 floating holidays per calendar year. Unused floating holidays do not carry over to the next year.

## 2. Vacation

Vacation accrues every month, by years of service:

| Years of service | Vacation days per year |
|------------------|------------------------|
| 0 to 2           | 15                     |
| 3 to 5           | 20                     |
| 6 or more        | 25                     |

Up to 5 unused vacation days carry over to the next year; the rest lapse on 31 December.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees have 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is needed for an absence of more than 3 consecutive working days.

## 4. Parental leave

Parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave, to be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## 5. Exceptions

Employees in their first year accrue vacation at the rate in section 2, but may not take more than 5 days before they complete 6 months of service.
