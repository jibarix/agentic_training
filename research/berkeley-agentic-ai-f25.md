# CS194/294-196 — Agentic AI (UC Berkeley, Fall 2025)

- **URL(s):** https://rdi.berkeley.edu/agentic-ai/f25 (course home + syllabus), MOOC version https://agenticai-learning.org/f25. Competition: https://rdi.berkeley.edu/agentx-agentbeats. Tutorial repo: https://github.com/RDI-Foundation/agentbeats-tutorial. YouTube playlist ID PLS01nW3RtgoqGkm4UeqNeZLccW-OGc1fJ (taken from the lecture links)
- **Instructors:** Dawn Song (UC Berkeley, instructor). Teaching staff: Xiuyu Li, Baifeng Shi, Chenyang Wang, Arhaan Aggarwal, Richik Pal. The MOOC page also lists Xinyun Chen (Meta) as guest co-instructor. Most lectures are given by external guest speakers.
- **Level/prereqs:** Undergraduate (CS194-196, class 15131) and graduate (CS294-196, class 32761). Prerequisite: machine learning and deep learning experience, e.g. CS182, CS188 or CS189.
- **Course format:** In person, Mondays 3:10-5:00pm PT, Valley Life Sciences 2050. Dec 1 (2:00-3:30pm PT) and Dec 8 (11:30am-1:30pm PT) were held at different times. Variable units. Grading: 1 unit is participation 40%, quizzes 20%, article 40%. 2 units is participation 30%, quizzes 10%, project 60%. 3/4 units is participation 20%, quizzes 10%, project 70%. Bonus credit for taking part in the AgentX-AgentBeats competition. Q&A is on Edstem. The MOOC ran the same lectures online with completion-graded quizzes (usually 5 multiple-choice questions each) and certificate tiers (Trailblazer, Mastery, Legendary, Honorary), all due Jan 31, 2026.
- **Public materials:** 13 scheduled sessions (Sep 8 to Dec 8, with no lecture Nov 24). Slide PDFs for 9 sessions. YouTube video for 12 sessions: 11 recordings plus 1 livestream link (Dec 8, safety). Supplemental readings for 7 sessions. There is no project spec document on the course page; the project is described only through the grading and timeline sections and the Oct 6 "Project Overview" lecture.
- **License:** None stated on the course page, the MOOC page or the tutorial repo.
- **Past offering:** Earlier MOOCs in the series: LLM Agents, Fall 2024 (https://agenticai-learning.org/f24), and Advanced LLM Agents, Spring 2025 (https://agenticai-learning.org/sp25).

**Overview.** The course covers LLM agents from foundations to deployment. Topics are LLM foundations, reasoning and planning, agent frameworks and infrastructure, applications (code generation, robotics, web automation, scientific discovery), and the limitations and risks of agents. Apart from Dawn Song's introduction and closing safety lecture, it is a guest-speaker series with speakers from OpenAI, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Meta, Stanford, Sierra, Google DeepMind and UT Austin/Sony AI. Recurring themes are post-training for agentic and verifiable tasks, agent evaluation and its statistics, multi-agent systems, and lessons from production deployment. The project (2+ units) is the distinctive part, and it is built around AgentBeats. In Phase 1, teams build a "green agent", an evaluator agent that turns a benchmark into an agent and defines tasks, environment and scoring. In Phase 2, they build a "white agent" that gets evaluated. For an agent builder, the draw is the evaluation-first framing: agents talk to each other over the A2A protocol, and the benchmark is itself an agent.
